## Known limitations

Not backlog items — things that were tried or judged and will not change without new information.

| Limitation | Tool | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Fixer assemblies load into the default `AssemblyLoadContext`, so their files stay mapped for the life of the process and `clean` can meet one | `cleanup fix=…`, `clean` | **Attempted in 0.15.0 and reverted.** Loading them through a collectible `AssemblyLoadContext` broke fixer discovery — `CodeFixServiceTests.Cleanup_WithAnalyzerFixes_AppliesTheAnalyzerCodeFix` went red because the exports stopped resolving across the context boundary. Shipping a broken `cleanup fix=analyzers` to close a row is strictly worse than the symptom. The stale-provider and unbounded-growth halves *are* fixed: the cache key carries each analyzer's `(path, last write, length)`, it is bounded at 32 entries, and `unload_workspace` clears it. Reopen only with a loader that shares Roslyn's own types. |
| A generic `detail=` verbosity knob for every listing tool | all listings | **Rejected.** `signatures`, `ids`, `containers` and `filter` already give per-tool control with names that say what they do; a generic `detail` would duplicate them and make each tool's cheapest form less discoverable. The repo rule is that improving an existing parameter beats adding a surface. `verbose` on `build`/`run_tests`/`format` is not this — it is a per-tool escape hatch from a *cheaper default*, not a knob you must find before the tool is useful. |
